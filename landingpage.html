Frequently asked questions

Is it free? +

Yes. The first 21-day batch is free.

How much time does it take? +

At least 30 minutes a day on your own goal.

What counts as proof? +

A screenshot, a link, a photo of your notes, a timer or workout log. No faces or personal details needed.

Do I need to show my face? +

No.

What if I miss a day? +

Come back the next day. Streaks can break, people shouldn't.

Who is it for? +

Builders, hustlers and doers: anyone working on a goal, whether that's a project, studies, fitness or a business. Students are very welcome.

How are shoutouts chosen? +

Every Sunday we look at who showed up with proof the most (Consistency Star), who helped others (Helper of the Week), and which win the community liked most (Win of the Week).

What kind of resources are shared? +

Free, hand-picked tutorials, guides, tools and templates for each field. Mods add them, and you can suggest more. Pirated or paid-course dumps aren't allowed.

Can I work on group projects? +

Yes. Members can team up in their field channels once the batch is underway.

Can I ask questions? +

Yes. There's a help channel, and no question is too small.

Who runs it? +

A CS student building this in public.
